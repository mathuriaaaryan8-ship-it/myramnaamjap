Last updated: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how myramnaamjap.in ("we", "our", "the website") handles information when you use our free online naam jap counter.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

4. Third-party services

We load fonts from Google Fonts. These services may receive your IP address when your browser requests the files. We have no control over their privacy practices; please review their own policies.

5. Children's privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Your choices

You can block or delete cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Doing so may remove your saved counts.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.

8. Contact
